Made by Hand, to Order

Keepsakes

A hand does not stay the size it is for very long. Klay Art Studio visits the home directly, takes the impression while it can still be taken, and returns it months later as fired, framed clay meant to last well beyond the childhood it recorded. What began as an afternoon becomes something a family keeps for generations.

Baby Hand and Foot Frame

Fired clay, wooden frame, 8 inch format

  1. You get in touch, by WhatsApp, phone, or email, to arrange a date.
  2. Klay visits your home. The impression is taken directly, while the hand or foot is still that size.
  3. The clay is fired, carried back to the studio and hardened permanently in the kiln.
  4. It is framed, set into a wood frame, ready to hang.
  5. It is delivered back to your door, finished.
