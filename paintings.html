Original Watercolours, One of One

Paintings

Two series occupy the studio's attention at present. The Complexity Series is given to machinery that has stopped working, tractors mostly, left standing in fields long after the last harvest they were useful for. The Shampan Series follows the wooden boats built and rebuilt along Bengal's rivers, painted before the tide or the shipbreakers take the ones still standing. A collector who wants neither may commission something else entirely. Every painting that leaves this studio was made once, for one wall, and will not be made again.
