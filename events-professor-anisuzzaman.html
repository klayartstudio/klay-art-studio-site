An Afternoon with Professor Anisuzzaman

Professor Anisuzzaman, a teacher and artist with decades behind him, spent an afternoon at Klay Art Studio working the wheel alongside its own hands. There was no lesson plan for the day, only clay, and the kind of conversation that happens naturally when two people who care about the same material are in a room together.

Klay Art Studio remains grateful for the time, and for what was learned simply by watching him work.
