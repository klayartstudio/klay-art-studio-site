About the Series

Faisal Md. Mahfuz paints what working machines leave behind once the work is finished. Tractors, mostly, standing in the fields that once depended on them, their surfaces worn into something no factory could reproduce. Each piece in the series is watercolour on paper, 40 by 20 centimetres, framed and sold exactly once.
