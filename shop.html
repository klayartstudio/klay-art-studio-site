The studio's kiln produces more than what appears on commission. Plates and showpieces, made in small numbers and rarely repeated, sit alongside DAHUK, a line of carved terracotta wall pieces named for a wetland bird that keeps close to the ground and is rarely seen. Each piece is available while the batch lasts, and not reordered once it sells through.
