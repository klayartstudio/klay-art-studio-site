Who Makes the Work

Artists

Klay Art Studio was never meant to hold one voice. It began with a ceramicist who wanted clay in more hands than his own, and it has grown by the same instinct since: gather people who take their material seriously, and stay out of their way. A printmaker keeps her own hours here alongside the studio's founder. Neither is asked to sound like the other, and the work is better for it.

Faisal Md. Mahfuz

Ceramic designer and founder of Klay Art Studio, painting the Complexity Series in the hours the kiln allows.
