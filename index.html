Paintings

The Complexity Series studies machinery no longer in use, tractors left to rust in the fields that once depended on them. The Shampan Series turns to the river, to wooden boats that have carried Bengal's coast for longer than anyone painting them today has been alive. Each work is painted once. None are printed again.

Experiences

Klay Art Studio does not yet keep a studio of its own. Instead, the wheel travels, to a private residence hosting its own gathering, to the courtyard of a company marking an occasion worth remembering with something other than catering. Guests rarely leave with what they expected to.

03

Keepsakes

A hand does not stay the size it is for very long. Klay Art Studio visits the home directly, takes the impression while it can still be taken, and returns it months later as fired, framed clay meant to last well beyond the childhood it recorded. What began as an afternoon becomes something a family keeps for generations.

Shop

Plates and showpieces are made from the same clay, fired in the same kiln, as everything else the studio produces. Among them sits DAHUK, a small collection of carved terracotta named for a wetland bird that keeps close to the ground and rarely calls attention to itself.
