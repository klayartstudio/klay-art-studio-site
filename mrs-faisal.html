The Shampan Series

Mrs Faisal follows the wooden boats of Bengal's coast, painted in layered watercolour before time takes the last of them. Every weathered plank and faded rope carries the rhythm of the rivers it worked. Each piece is 50 by 38 centimetres, framed, and will not be repeated.
