Series · Rumana Rahman

Silent Bloom: Nature in the Time of Covid-19

Rumana Rahman Collagraph, 13 × 17 cm
01

About the Series

During the global Covid-19 lockdown, while human life was constrained and cities fell silent, nature seemed to breathe again. With less pollution, quieter roads, and reduced industrial activity, the Earth's quiet resurgence became visible — clearer skies, birds returning to the cities, a stillness that felt both unfamiliar and comforting.

This body of work explores the poetic contrast between human isolation and the quiet flourishing of nature. Using the medium of collagraph, Rumana Rahman captures moments of still beauty — textured leaves, expansive skies, wild growth amid urban decay. The sharp lines and tactile surfaces mirror both nature's fragility and its strength, reflecting on how the pause in human activity revealed an alternative vision of the world: one slower, gentler, and deeply interconnected.
